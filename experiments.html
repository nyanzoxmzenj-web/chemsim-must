MUST laboratory safety rules

Follow these COSTE Applied Sciences protocols before every virtual or physical practical.

1
Lab coats at all timesWear a fastened laboratory coat from entry to exit. Bare arms are not permitted at the MUST bench.
2
Safety goggles requiredEye protection stays on during heating, titration, distillation and any transfer of reagents.
3
No eating or drinkingFood, drinks and cosmetics are forbidden in the laboratory to prevent chemical ingestion.
4
Proper pipetting onlyUse a pipette filler or bulb. Mouth pipetting is strictly prohibited under MUST safety policy.
5
Acid into waterAlways add acid to water, slowly and with stirring. Never add water to concentrated acid.
