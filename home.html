MUST laboratory safety

Required conduct for NSLT 07406 practical work, on campus and in ChemSim.

  • Lab coats and safety goggles at all times.
  • Closed-toe shoes only; no sandals or open footwear.
  • No eating or drinking in the laboratory.
  • Never mouth-pipette; use bulbs or pipettors.
  • Add acid into water, never the reverse.
  • Waft vapours toward the nose; do not sniff directly.
  • Report accidents, spills, and injuries immediately.
  • Use a fume hood for volatiles and irritating fumes.
